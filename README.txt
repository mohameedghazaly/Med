MedTrack for iPhone (installable web app)

1. Put this whole folder online on any free static host. Easiest: open app.netlify.com/drop on a computer and drag the folder (or the unzipped folder) onto the page. You get an https link.
2. On the iPhone, open that link in Safari (not in another app).
3. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
4. Open MedTrack from the Home Screen. It runs full screen and works offline after the first load.

Notes
- Your data is saved on the phone. Use Today > Your data > Backup and restore now and then, and paste the text into Notes.
- This version has no Ask Claude, Plan my day, photo upload or PDF attach. Those work in the Claude version of MedTrack.
- After you replace the files on the host, open the app twice to pick up the update.
